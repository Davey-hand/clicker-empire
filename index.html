How to play
Computer: Use the arrow keys or A and D to move. Press Space, W, or the Up Arrow to jump.
Phone or tablet: Hold the arrow buttons to move and the up button to jump.
Collect gold coins, jump across platforms, and avoid the red enemies. Falling or touching an enemy ends the run. Your best score saves on this device.
